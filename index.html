§ 01 About

The interesting work in data isn't the pipeline — it's everything around it.

The contracts upstream, the modelling in the middle, the lineage and observability that make the system legible, and the automation that keeps it honest.

That's where I spend my time, and increasingly, on the harder problem alongside it: translating what the engineering makes possible into outcomes the business can act on.

AI sits inside the practice, not on top of it — Claude Code as a collaborator at the keyboard, well-mannered agents where they add signal rather than noise.
